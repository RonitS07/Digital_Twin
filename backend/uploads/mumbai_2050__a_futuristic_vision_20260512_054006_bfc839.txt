The transportation system in Mumbai 2050 is a marvel of innovation. The city's streets are lined with electric and self-driving vehicles, reducing congestion and pollution. Mumbai 2050 is a city that runs on clean energy. The city's energy needs are met by a combination of solar, wind, and hydrogen power. The city's water supply is also sustainable, with a system of rainwater harvesting and recycling that ensures every drop is used efficiently.

The city's parks and gardens are designed to be sustainable and self-sufficient, with green roofs and walls that help to reduce the urban heat island effect. The city's waterfront has also been transformed into a vibrant public space, with walking and cycling paths, public art installations, and community gardens.

Mumbai 2050 is a city that is poised to become a model for sustainable urban development. With its innovative architecture, efficient transportation system, and commitment to clean energy and water, this city is a beacon of hope for a brighter future.
