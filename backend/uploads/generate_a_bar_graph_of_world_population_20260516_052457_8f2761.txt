# World Population Bar Graph

## Introduction

The world population has been increasing steadily over the years, with a significant growth rate in the past century. This bar graph provides a visual representation of the world population from 1950 to 2020, highlighting the growth trends and patterns.

## Data

The data used for this graph is based on the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs Population Division's estimates and projections.

### Source:

- United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs Population Division (2020)

### Data:

| Year | World Population (in millions) |
| --- | --- |
| 1950 | 2,555 |
| 1960 | 3,025 |
| 1970 | 3,692 |
| 1980 | 4,453 |
| 1990 | 5,310 |
| 2000 | 6,143 |
| 2010 | 6,909 |
| 2020 | 7,794 |

## Bar Graph

Here is the bar graph representing the world population from 1950 to 2020:

```
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

years = [1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1990, 2000, 2010, 2020]
population = [2555, 3025, 3692, 4453, 5310, 6143, 6909, 7794]

plt.bar(years, population)
plt.xlabel('Year')
plt.ylabel('World Population (in millions)')
plt.title('World Population Bar Graph')
plt.show()
```

Note: This is a Python code snippet to generate a bar graph. You can use any other programming language or tool to create a similar graph.

## Conclusion

The bar graph shows a steady increase in the world population over the years, with a significant growth rate in the past century. The graph highlights the importance of population growth and its impact on the environment, economy, and society.
